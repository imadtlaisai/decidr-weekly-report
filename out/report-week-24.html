Key observations
1
DecidrOS quality is strong — ~72% MQL rate. 62 of 86 qualified-track DecidrOS contacts reached MQL, with 5 SQL+ and both opportunities. The DecidrOS funnel is healthy.
2
Sugarworks lead quality is the watch item — 19% MQL rate. 76 new Sugarworks leads but only 18 reached MQL and none progressed to SQL. Audience / targeting review recommended.
3
"AI Roadmap" demo campaign converts at 100% MQL. Every demo-request lead from this Facebook AU campaign qualified, and it produced both opportunities (Heath Hebenton, Lori K). Highest-intent source this week.
4
LinkedIn out-qualifies Facebook. LinkedIn 62% MQL rate (18 of 29) vs Facebook 40% (52 of 180). LinkedIn is the cleaner-fit, lower-volume channel; Facebook is the volume engine.
5
FB Singapore AI Readiness underperforms — 28 contacts, 11% MQL. Consistent with low-fit geo targeting. Audience / geo review and possible budget reallocation worth considering.
6
Channel concentration: Paid Social is 79% of volume (209 of 266). Only Direct (29) and Offline (28) contribute otherwise. Diversifying source mix would reduce single-channel risk.
7
Paid media: $28.8k spent, $138 blended cost / lead. LinkedIn runs ~6× Facebook per lead ($455 vs $71); Google spent $2.8k with no HubSpot-attributed leads — paid-search lead capture is the clearest efficiency fix.
8
Attribution gap: 192 of 266 contacts have no country. Mainly Facebook Lead Ads. Capturing country on-form would sharpen geo reporting.
